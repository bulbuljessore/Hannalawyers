A conveyancer can handle a standard transaction: the documents, the bank, settlement. A property lawyer does all of that and can also advise when something goes wrong, such as a complicated contract, a dispute, or an unusual ownership arrangement.

Talk to us before you sign the contract of sale, because once you sign it you are bound by it. When we go through the contract we also cover the grants you may be eligible for, pest and building reports, and what happens at each step.
